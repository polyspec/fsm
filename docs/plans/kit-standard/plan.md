# Adopt the kit standard 0.0.54

[한국어](plan.ko.md)

`fsm-<package>-<task>` that the fixed kit tasks join by prerequisites (K246-2), the gates of PRINCIPLES.md, and the PHP version
of the owner ruling of 2026-10-19 (PHP 8.4). Its item records the checks that do not pass and why.
